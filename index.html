The Problem

Antarctic navigation is a $2B+ blind spot

As polar shipping routes open up due to climate change, more vessels are transiting Antarctic waters — but navigation tools haven't kept pace. Current systems show a single predicted iceberg path, ignoring the massive uncertainty in drift forecasts.

🧊

30% More Incursions

Large iceberg incursions into shipping lanes have increased 30% over the past decade, with major events like A23a threatening critical trade routes.

⚠️

Single-Path Blindness

Existing models predict one iceberg trajectory. In reality, ocean currents, wind, and temperature create a fan of possible paths — and the danger zone is much wider than shown.

🌊

Multi-Threat Complexity

Real scenarios involve multiple icebergs and multiple ships simultaneously. No current tool evaluates fleet-wide risk across all ship-berg pairs in real time.

Our Solution

Monte Carlo uncertainty meets live fleet monitoring

ICE·SIGHT FLEET combines probabilistic drift simulation, real-time weather integration, and machine learning to give operators a complete risk picture.

🎲

Monte Carlo Drift Ensemble

Each iceberg gets 7+ plausible drift paths under forecast uncertainty, showing the full cone of possible positions — not just one.

Core Engine
🌤️

Live Weather Integration

Pulls real wind and temperature data from Open-Meteo and feeds it into the drift model. Works offline with intelligent fallback.

API Connected
🚢

Multi-Ship Fleet Routing
